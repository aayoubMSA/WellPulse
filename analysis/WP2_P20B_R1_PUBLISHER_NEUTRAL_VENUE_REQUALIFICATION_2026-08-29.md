## 1. Trigger

The initial P20B matrix was scientifically reasoned but publisher-concentrated. Its candidate universe was inherited from an Elsevier-heavy historical shortlist and was not sufficiently publisher-neutral. This is a material venue-selection risk because a strong journal family can be missed even when the selected Elsevier venue itself is valid.

This bounded requalification reopens only the venue-universe risk class. It does not reopen experiments, P13 claims, P17/P17V conclusions, P18RC figures, P19 artifact semantics or P20A novelty.

## 2. Corrective doctrine

A defensible venue search must:

1. begin from the manuscript's current scientific identity, not a historical publisher shortlist;
2. screen multiple serious publisher families where plausible;
3. test current official scope/policies;
4. test actual recent publications, not scope keywords alone;
5. distinguish best scientific/prestige target from best lower-burden/acceptance-efficiency target;
6. preserve author preference only as a tiebreaker after scientific fit passes;
7. keep author commitment, rights, APC/payment and submission separate downstream decisions.

## 4. Key current evidence

### IEEE Internet of Things Journal — very strong fit

Current official IoT-J scope includes:

- IoT architectures;
- embedded software;
- communication and networking protocols;
- IoT services/applications;
- IoT test-beds, trials and experiments;
- system security/manageability.

Current official journal page reports average submission-to-first-decision of about 6.9 weeks and submission-to-ePublication of about 14.5 weeks.

Recent publication evidence confirms that the journal publishes MQTT-centered work, resilience studies, real-device/testbed experiments and end-to-end IoT systems work.

Cost constraint: traditional/hybrid publication is available without an OA APC, but mandatory overlength charges are $175 per published page beyond the first 8 pages. This is a real burden risk for the current manuscript and must be modeled during P20D if this venue is chosen.

### ACM Transactions on Internet of Things — very strong scientific fit

ACM TIOT explicitly emphasizes:

- system designs;
- end-to-end architectures;
- enabling technologies;
- non-functional properties including dependability, timeliness, robustness, security and privacy;
- IoT testbeds;
- strong experimental corroboration.

This maps unusually well to WellPulse's failure-domain-aware, experimentally grounded contribution. Current 2026 publications include MQTT-based IoT work and experimentally evaluated IoT reliability/system papers.

ACM transitioned to a fully open-access publishing model in 2026. Institutional ACM Open participation and any title-specific APC obligation must therefore be checked before commitment. Cost status is not yet assumed.

### IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management — strong but narrower fit

TNSM explicitly covers reliability and quality assurance, management architectures/frameworks, applications/case studies, performance evaluation and network/service management. Current guidance targets author notification in under roughly 70 days. Up to 10 final two-column pages are free; excess pages are charged at $220/page, with a maximum of 16 pages.

Fit risk: WellPulse is not fundamentally a network-management paper, so a TNSM submission would require stronger management/fault-observability framing than IoT-J or TIOT.

### IEEE Open Journal of the Communications Society — technically strong, cost-heavy

OJ-COMS covers systems/network architecture, protocols/software/middleware, QoS, reliability/security, IoT/edge/cloud and practical experiments/prototypes. It is fully open access, has no page limit, and advertises rapid peer review. The 2026 APC is $2,160 before applicable discounts/agreements/taxes.

This is scientifically viable but economically weaker unless institutional OA funding is available.

### Elsevier Internet of Things — strongest lower-burden general IoT route

The journal directly covers IoT reliability, Full Research, Open Software and Data, case studies and timely publication. It remains an excellent scientific match and likely requires less transformation than network-management or architecture-specialist journals.

Its position changes from unquestioned primary to strong publisher-neutral fallback because IEEE IoT-J and ACM TIOT were omitted from the original candidate universe.

### Computer Networks — strong networking backup

Strong legitimacy and current MQTT/intermittent-IoT publication evidence, but more network/protocol-performance centered than the current compound evidence-methodology contribution.

### Journal of Systems Architecture — conditional architecture backup

Good fit for embedded software/system architecture, but POWDER physical-path evidence would need a more architecture-forward narrative.

### Springer Journal of Network and Systems Management — viable but not leading

Current publications demonstrate resilience, monitoring, configuration and network-systems management work. However, the journal is less directly IoT-centered than IoT-J, TIOT or Elsevier Internet of Things, and offers no clear utility advantage for the current paper.

## 7. IEEE-specific risk analysis

### IoT-J

Main risk is not scope. It is editorial bar + final-length economics.

P20D, if IoT-J is author-selected, must first perform a lossless IEEE-length simulation:

- IEEE two-column conversion;
- estimate final published page count;
- preserve all four main figures only if legible;
- move nonessential detail to supplement/artifact without weakening caveats;
- estimate mandatory overlength cost before any submission commitment.

If the validated scientific narrative cannot fit acceptably near 8 pages without destructive compression, the fallback should be Elsevier Internet of Things rather than silently paying or damaging the paper.

### TNSM

TNSM offers 10 pages before overlength charges and strong reliability/fault-management fit, but only becomes preferable if the manuscript can be honestly reframed around network/service-management observability and recovery semantics without distorting the current compound contribution.

### OJ-COMS

No page limit is attractive, but the 2026 mandatory OA APC makes it a conditional economic route rather than a first choice unless institutional coverage exists.

## 8. ACM-specific risk analysis

ACM TIOT may be one of the best pure scientific fits in the entire set because it explicitly values end-to-end IoT architectures, robustness/dependability, testbeds and strong experiments.

However, P20B-R1 does not assume MSA University participates in ACM Open. That institutional/APC question must be checked before author commitment.
