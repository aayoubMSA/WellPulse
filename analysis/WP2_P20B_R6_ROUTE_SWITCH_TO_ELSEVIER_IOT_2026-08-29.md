## Trigger

The author raised a new decision objective after P21 preparation: **rapid editorial triage / fast desk acceptance-rejection feedback**. This is materially different from average submission-to-first-decision time and was underweighted in the prior R5 decision.

## Decision

The first submission route is changed from **IEEE Internet of Things Journal** to **Internet of Things (Elsevier)**.

IEEE IoT-J remains the principal specialist backup.

No science is reopened. No experiment, result, claim, P13/P17/P17V conclusion, P18RC figure authority or P19 reviewer-artifact authority is changed by this route switch.

## Current official venue-fit evidence

Elsevier currently describes *Internet of Things* as a cross-disciplinary venue for researchers, engineers and practitioners across the breadth of IoT and explicitly states that the journal places **high priority on timely publication**. It lists **Full Research papers**, **Survey Papers**, **Open Software and Data**, tutorials/best practices and case studies among supported article forms. The journal scope explicitly includes IoT reliability and engineering/software aspects.

The selected article type for WellPulse remains:

**Full Research paper**

The manuscript is not relabeled as a Survey Paper.

## Survey-visibility objective

The author also required that the publication properly show the substantial literature/novelty-survey effort already completed.

The venue switch therefore includes a production requirement:

- report the structured submission-date comparator survey explicitly in the main manuscript;
- show the seven search axes;
- report the 17 source/axis groups;
- report 11 wording-narrowing outcomes, six no-impact outcomes and zero scientific blockers;
- include a comparator-to-claim-consequence table;
- explain how the survey narrowed the claim envelope;
- do **not** call the embedded survey a PRISMA/systematic review because it was conducted as a novelty/positioning control rather than an independent systematic-review protocol.

This makes literature-positioning effort visible without changing the paper from empirical Full Research into a review article.
