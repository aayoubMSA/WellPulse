# Evidence Boundary

Remote-testbed results may support claims about embedded execution, message persistence, connectivity degradation, MQTT/session recovery, edge/cloud behavior, packet/message delivery, latency, reconnection, buffering, and reconciliation when those variables are physically exercised.

They do **not** by themselves support claims about pump mechanics, hydraulic behavior, Siwa groundwater, motor or bearing faults, crop physiology, local agronomic performance, or Siwa-specific environmental conditions.

## Language rule

Use: **real embedded-hardware validation under controlled connectivity impairment** after WP-RT01 succeeds.

Use: **real outdoor rural wireless validation** only after an ARA experiment actually succeeds.

Do not use: **Siwa field validated** until the WellPulse gateway is physically deployed at the relevant Siwa installation and corresponding field evidence is preserved.
