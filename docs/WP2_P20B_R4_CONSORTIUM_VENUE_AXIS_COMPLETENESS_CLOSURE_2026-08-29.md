## Closure result

The consortium found four additional material venue-selection dimensions beyond the four already active. The final venue decision must therefore use exactly eight weighted axes:

1. Scientific fit & editorial/reviewer plausibility.
2. Academic/institutional recognition & field signaling.
3. Industrial Pull / Translational Discoverability.
4. Audience reach, access & citation discoverability.
5. Economics & route certainty.
6. Speed & process predictability.
7. Rights, IP/control & commercialization optionality.
8. Evidence-package preservation & reproducibility.

Recommended default WellPulse weights:
`25 / 10 / 15 / 10 / 10 / 10 / 10 / 10`.

Strategic option value for future grants/collaborators/standards/recruitment is retained only as a tiebreaker to avoid double-counting A2–A4.

Legitimacy, COPE/ethics, AI policy, plagiarism/originality, privacy/security, accessibility, correction/retraction, digital preservation, special-issue risk and disclosure compatibility remain pass/fail due-diligence gates, not default weighted axes.

## Material new implication

Academic/institutional recognition must be measured explicitly for the Egyptian context rather than inferred from generic prestige. Current SCU guidance uses year-specific Web of Science/Scopus classifications and subject categories, and current cycle-15 rules make qualifying WoS Q1/Q2 status materially relevant.

Rights/IP/control must also be measured separately from price. Preprint, accepted-manuscript sharing, embargo, copyright/licence, patent-right retention and commercial-reuse conditions can change strategic value even when scientific fit and APC are identical.
