Dear Editor,

Please consider the Research Article entitled \textbf{Beyond Reconnection: Measuring Recovery Observables and Replay-Induced Continuity Gaps in MQTT Telemetry} for publication in \textit{Computer Communications}.

The manuscript presents an experimental network/IoT measurement study that separates recovery into distinct observables for network-path restoration, MQTT-session restoration, sender-to-broker QoS-1 acknowledgement closure, receiver-side historical completion, live-data continuity, and receiver ordering. The study combines controlled FIT IoT-LAB experiments with complementary POWDER observations without pooling the two testbeds into one effect estimate.

The principal empirical result is deliberately bounded. In the tested serialized W1 replay implementation, eventual receiver completeness was preserved while generation of the first post-backlog record was suspended for tens of seconds. A prospective three-run campaign then instrumented broker-side acknowledgement closure and receiver completion directly; all three runs remained unresolved within the conservative timing bounds, and the manuscript retains that negative result rather than converting it into a favorable recovery claim.

This positioning matches the journal's interest in experimental testbeds, Internet-of-Things communication, and measurement/evaluation of computer communication networks. The manuscript does not claim a new MQTT recovery architecture, universal durability-versus-freshness behavior, or pooled cross-testbed reliability.

A sanitized reproducibility supplement accompanies the submission. It contains the execution-configuration matrix, exact code-authority manifest, clock-bound procedure, and deterministic self-check while excluding credentials and private infrastructure identifiers. The manuscript also contains the applicable generative-AI disclosure.

The manuscript is a single-author work, is not currently under consideration by another journal, and has not been published previously. This work received no external research funding, and the author declares no known competing financial interests or personal relationships that could have appeared to influence the work.
