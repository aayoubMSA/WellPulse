# POWDER G5 RF Calibration — +45/+50/+55 dB Fine Sweep

Date: 2026-08-26 (Cairo)
Experiment: WP-G5-RF-CAL
Purpose: non-scored WP2 fine RF calibration near the LTE threshold.

## Context
The prior sweep established monotonic RF behavior through +40 dB additional attenuation. This fine sweep examined +45, +50, and +55 dB, followed by an automatic reset to +40 dB.

## Observed radio behavior

### +45 dB additional attenuation
- RSRP settled near -104 dBm.
- DL SNR approximately 14 dB.
- DL MCS approximately 8.
- DL BLER was usually 0%, with at least one transient 50% sample.
- Link remained active.

Interpretation: clearly degraded but still operational; a strong Q1 candidate.

### +50 dB additional attenuation
- RSRP settled near -107 dBm.
- DL SNR approximately 10–11 dB, occasionally around 6 dB.
- DL MCS approximately 5.9–6.0.
- DL BLER was usually 0%, with transient 50% samples.
- Link remained active.

Interpretation: near-threshold behavior begins; candidate boundary between Q1 and Q2.

### +55 dB additional attenuation
- RSRP approximately -109 to -110 dBm.
- DL SNR approximately 7.8–8.5 dB.
- DL MCS collapsed from about 5–6 to approximately 0.7–0.9 in later samples.
- DL bitrate fell to roughly 256–264 bps-equivalent reported values in the captured metrics, with intermittent zero reported DL bitrate samples during transition.
- DL BLER remained mostly 0% in the captured samples, but the radio was visibly operating at the edge.

Interpretation: strong Q2 candidate, but packet-level intermittency/outage must still be verified before freezing Q2 or Q3.

## Recovery check
After automatic reset to +40 dB, RSRP returned to about -100 dBm and DL SNR to about 18 dB, confirming reversible RF control and successful recovery.

## Gate
Fine sweep: PASS as calibration evidence.

No semantic Q2/Q3 freeze yet because the uploaded packet log did not include enough stage-aligned ping samples to prove intermittent packet loss or effective application-data outage at +55 dB.

This is non-scored calibration evidence only; it does not authorize scored runs.
