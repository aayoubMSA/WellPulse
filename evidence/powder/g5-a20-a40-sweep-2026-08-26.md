# POWDER G5 RF Calibration — +20 to +40 dB Sweep

Date: 2026-08-26 (Cairo)
Experiment: WP-G5-RF-CAL
Purpose: non-scored WP2 RF calibration sweep after validation of Q0 and +10 dB response.

## Bound resources
- enb1 -> nuc1 (network/eNB side)
- rue1 -> nuc2 (UE side)
- RFLink path: `1,33,2,34:nuc2/nuc1`

## Sweep protocol
All four programmable attenuator IDs were set together. A continuous ping from `nuc2` to EPC SGi `172.16.0.1` remained active while srsUE live metrics were logged.

## Observed RF points
- +20 dB additional attenuation: RSRP about -81 dBm, DL SNR about 34–35 dB, DL MCS about 20, DL BLER 0%, UL BLER 0%. Strong/stable.
- +30 dB additional attenuation: RSRP about -91 dBm, DL SNR about 27 dB, DL MCS about 20, DL BLER 0%, UL BLER 0%. Degraded but stable.
- +40 dB additional attenuation: RSRP converged to about -100 dBm, DL SNR about 18–19 dB, DL MCS about 12, DL BLER 0%, UL BLER 0%. Clearly degraded but still radio-stable.

The sweep remained monotonic: increasing programmed attenuation reduced RSRP and SNR and lowered DL MCS as expected.

## Data-plane continuity
A captured continuous-ping tail spanning sequence numbers 433 through 512 showed consecutive replies with no gaps in the displayed interval. RTTs in that interval were roughly 18.6–45.9 ms. No application-data outage was observed through +40 dB in the captured tail.

## Interpretation
- +20 dB is still too strong for a semantic degraded-state freeze.
- +30 dB is a plausible Q1 candidate because radio quality is degraded while connectivity and BLER remain stable.
- +40 dB is materially degraded but remains well connected; it is not yet a near-threshold/outage state.
- Fine calibration above +40 dB is required before freezing Q2/Q3.

## Gate
+20/+30/+40 dB non-scored calibration sweep: PASS.

No scored-run authorization is implied. Semantic Q1/Q2/Q3 values remain provisional pending fine-threshold calibration.
